Four moves, then a loop.

Nocturne is a workflow, not a wall of knobs. You can have something playing inside a minute, and the depth is there when you want it.

  1. 1.0

    Tap the grid.

    Each row is a drum, each column a sixteenth note. Tap the squares where you want a hit. The kick on one, the snare on five and thirteen — the classics are two taps away.

  2. 2.0

    Set the tempo.

    Drag the tempo slider from a slow 60 to a brisk 160. The playhead sweeps the grid in time, so you always see which step is sounding.

  3. 3.0

    Sit in it.

    The loop repeats until you stop it. Mute a track, add a hat, take the clap out again. Nothing commits — the pattern is yours to keep rearranging.

  4. 4.0

    Share the link.

    When a pattern is worth keeping, copy the address. The grid packs into the URL, so a friend opens the same beat in their own tab — no upload, no file, no sign-in.

One kit, voiced for the dark.

Every sound is synthesised live in the tab — no samples to download. Four voices, tuned warm and a little dry, so the loop sits low enough to play at 2 a.m. without waking the building.

  • Track 1

    Kick

    A short sine drop with a touch of click on the transient. Tuned to 55 Hz so it reads as weight, not boom, through laptop speakers.

  • Track 2

    Snare

    Filtered noise over a tuned body. Snappy, never harsh.

  • Track 3

    Hat

    High-passed noise with a fast decay. The metronome of the loop.

  • Track 4

    Clap

    Three noise bursts a few milliseconds apart, so it lands like a room full of hands instead of one. Drop it on the off-beats and the loop opens up.

It lives in the tab.

There is no app to install and no project to manage. Nocturne is one page that happens to make sound. Close the tab and the room goes quiet; open it tomorrow and start again.

Nothing to install
The whole instrument is HTML, CSS, and the Web Audio API your browser already ships. Open the link and it runs.
Silent until you press play
Audio waits for your first tap on play — the way browsers want it, and the way a late-night tool should behave.
Keyboard all the way
Tab to any square, press space or enter to toggle a hit. Space over the play button starts and stops the loop.
The pattern is the URL
Your grid encodes into the address bar. Bookmark a beat, or paste it to a friend — no server keeps a copy.
