Built for one tab.

  1. 01

    Browser-first

    Everything runs in the tab. No installation, no sign-up wall, no sample packs to download. Open it, make a loop, close it.

  2. 02

    Five voices, synthesized

    Kick, snare, hi-hat, tom, and clap are generated with the Web Audio API — not sampled, not tracked, not compressed.

  3. 03

    Fast enough to jam

    A lookahead scheduler keeps timing tight, a tempo slider runs 60–180 BPM, and the master gain is right here in the surface.
