The Slow-Web Movement

A manifesto · 2026

The Web Is Not a Feed

We are building a web that loads at human speed, respects attention, and outlasts the cycle. Speed is not a virtue when it only serves to consume more.

Read the tenets
  1. 01

    We refuse infinite scroll.

    Infinite scroll turns visitors into livestock. A slow site ends — and that ending is the point.

  2. 02

    We design for readers, not engagement.

    Engagement is a debt you take out against your reader's attention. We make pages that can be closed and remembered.

  3. 03

    We publish complete thoughts.

    A thought published in fragments is not a thought; it is bait. We write whole arguments, then let them stand still.

  4. 04

    We own our archives.

    Platforms delete, deprecate, and decay. We keep our words in files we control, on domains we pay for, in formats that last.

  5. 05

    We load only what serves the sentence.

    Every script, tracker, and modal is a request for patience we have not earned. If it does not serve the sentence, it does not ship.

  6. 06

    We keep comments asynchronous.

    Comments are letters, not chat. We answer when we have something to say, and we give our readers room to do the same.

  7. 07

    We respect the link.

    The link is the original protocol of generosity. We cite sources, preserve URLs, and refuse to trap a reader inside one site.

  8. 08

    We build pages that sleep.

    A page should be able to rest. We build without real-time dashboards, persistent sockets, or the hum of always-on.

  9. 09

    We write for strangers ten years from now.

    Fashions pass. We write for the stranger who finds this years from today and still understands what we meant.

  10. 10

    We choose slowness as a feature.

    We do not mistake lag for failure. Sometimes the page is slow because the decision behind it is careful.
