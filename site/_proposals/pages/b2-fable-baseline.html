Inside the four voices

No sample packs, no licences. Each voice is built from oscillators and filtered noise, and every number below is live in this page's source — the spec is the implementation.

Kick

Sine wave, 150 Hz falling to 42 Hz over 120 ms · 400 ms decay

Snare

190 Hz triangle body under noise band-passed at 1.8 kHz · 180 ms tail

Hat

White noise through an 8 kHz high-pass · 55 ms gate

Clap

Three noise bursts 12 ms apart, band-passed at 1.5 kHz

Quiet by design

Lowlight holds the master gain at −8 dB and keeps every voice under half a second, so a pattern reads as a sketch, not a performance. There is no cloud and no save button: your loop lives in this tab's memory and vanishes when you close it. If it's worth keeping, record it — the machine won't hold it for you.
