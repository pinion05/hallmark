hallmark variants · interactive demo (no server)

What you are looking at

This is a client-only mock of the real hallmark variants picker. One brief, three structurally distinct directions, side by side in your own browser. In a real run each card is a live full-size page; here they are simple CSS mocks so the loop is the point, not the pixels.

The cards fill in one at a time: that is progressive reveal. The real picker writes each direction to the manifest as generating, then flips it to ready the moment its files land, so the first direction is viewable after roughly one direction's worth of work instead of waiting for all three.

Click a card, or press 1 / 2 / 3, to open a direction full-bleed: the page fills the viewport and every control lives in the small corner dock, bottom right, out of the design's way. Arrow keys (or the dock's numbers) flip between directions on the same spot, so you compare by muscle memory rather than by hunting tabs. Pick keeps one direction: only the winner gets finished and shipped. Graft composes, it takes one section from another direction into the winner (try "pricing from 3"). And in a real run a plain chat reply naming a direction is always a valid pick, so the flow never dead-ends.
