Sheet B · signal, storage, power

Three things that end a take.

Inputs, cards, and cells. The FR-2 is built so none of the three is the reason you missed it.

Input 2 ch

Two combo inputs

XLR and 1/4" on the same jack, +48 V phantom per channel, pad and high-pass switched in hardware.

Storage 512 GB

Card, plus a mirror

microSDXC to 512 GB, roughly 3 hours per GB at 48 k / 24-bit, with a mirrored safety track when you want it.

Power 11 h

Cells or the cable

Two AA or USB-C PD, about eleven hours continuous, hot-swapped on the dock without dropping the file.
