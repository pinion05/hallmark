How it's made

From wet clay to a fired vessel.

01

Thrown

Every form is thrown by hand on the wheel, then left to firm up on the board before it is trimmed and its foot is cut.

02

Glazed

Glazes are mixed in the studio from raw minerals and ash, then brushed or poured a piece at a time. Nothing is sprayed.

03

Fired

Pieces are packed close in a small kiln and fired slowly overnight, then left to cool for a full day before it is opened.
