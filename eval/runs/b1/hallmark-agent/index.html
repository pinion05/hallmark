03 · consent

No clone without a spoken yes.

A voice is not a font. Every clone at timbre starts with the speaker’s own consent, recorded in their own words.

01

The speaker records a consent clip on camera, stating their name and agreement, before a clone is trained.

02

We match the consent clip’s voice against the training sample. A mismatch stops the clone before it starts.

03

Every generated clip carries an inaudible watermark, so a clone can always be traced back to its source.

04

The speaker can revoke and delete their voice at any time. Deletion removes the model, not just the account.
