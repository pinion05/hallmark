02Process

From sample to studio in four steps.

No studio time, no engineer, no waiting on a callback. Four steps, start to finished audio.

01

Upload a sample

Six seconds is enough to start. Two to six minutes gets a near-perfect match. A voice memo, a podcast clip, an old interview: any clean recording works.

02

We model the voice

Formant's network isolates pitch, timbre, cadence, and breath pattern, then builds a private model that belongs only to your account.

03

Write or paste a script

Type anything, or paste a full chapter. Adjust pace, emphasis, and emotion with simple controls, no audio engineering required.

04

Generate in 40+ languages

The model keeps the voice and swaps the language, accent included. Export as WAV or MP3, or stream it live through the API.

05Trust & safety

Consent isn't a checkbox here. It's the architecture.

Voice cloning only earns trust if it is built to deserve it. Here is what runs under every clone on Formant.

Verified consent

Every cloned voice requires a recorded consent statement from the speaker before we train a single model.

Inaudible watermarking

Every generated clip carries an embedded watermark that traces back to the account that created it.

Opt-out registry

Public figures and voice actors can pre-register to block their voice from ever being cloned on Formant.

Revocable, always

Delete a voice model and every future clip loses the ability to generate from it, instantly.
