Common questions

Do my customers need to do anything different?

No. They keep emailing the same address they always have. Relay Desk changes what your team sees, not what they see.

Is Relay Desk live, or still being built?

It’s live. We’re early and don’t have case studies to point to yet, just a tool built the way we describe on this page. Start a queue and see for yourself.

What happens to our current shared inbox?

You keep it. Relay Desk organizes what comes through the address you already use. Nothing to migrate, nothing to export later.

How many people can share a queue?

As many as your team needs. Add teammates as you grow past what a shared inbox can handle.
