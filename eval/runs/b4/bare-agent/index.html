The problem

A shared inbox is not a system.

It's just a room everyone can walk into. Here's what that usually looks like by week two.

01

Who's on this?

Two teammates draft a reply to the same message because neither could tell the other already had it open.

02

Lost in the scroll

A message that needed an answer slides down the list and out of sight, until the customer follows up, annoyed.

03

No sense of what's open

There's no way to see what's outstanding, what's urgent, or who owns what, without opening every thread by hand.

04

Context lives in forwards

Looping in a teammate means forwarding the whole thread and hoping they read it before they hit reply.

The fix

Same inbox. A completely different way of working.

Relay Desk sits on top of the inbox your team already uses and turns every message into something you can see, claim, and track together.

How it works

Up and running before your coffee's cold.

No migration, no new inbox for customers to learn. Just structure, layered on top of what you already have.

01

Connect your inbox

Forward support@yourcompany.com to Relay Desk, or add us as an alias. It takes about five minutes, and nothing changes for your customers.

02

Messages become queue items

Every email becomes a card your team can see: who it's from, what it's about, and how long it's been waiting.

03

Claim it, assign it, discuss it

One click to say "I've got this," or hand it to a teammate. Leave internal notes right on the thread, invisible to the customer.

04

Track it to resolved

Every item carries a status: open, pending, or resolved, so anyone on the team can see where things stand without asking.

What's inside

Everything a small support team needs.

No ticket forms to configure, no seat-based sprawl. If your team can use email, they can use this.

Shared queues

See every open message in one ordered view, instead of six people refreshing the same inbox.

Claim and assign

One click to take a message, or hand it to a teammate. Ownership is always visible to the whole team.

Internal notes

Pull a teammate into a thread privately. The customer only ever sees the reply, never the conversation behind it.

Status at a glance

Open, pending, resolved, snoozed. Know what needs attention without opening a single thread.

Keep your inbox

Works with the email address you already give customers. Nothing to migrate, nothing new to announce.

A clock on every message

See how long something has been waiting, so nothing quietly ages into a complaint.

Why we're building this

Built for small teams, by a small team.

Relay Desk started the ordinary way: our own small team kept losing track of replies in an inbox built for one person, not five. Every tool we tried was sized for hundred-person support departments, with the setup time and price tag to match. We wanted something a small team could turn on in an afternoon and actually keep using, so that's what we're building, closely, with the first teams who join us.

Questions

Answers before you ask.

Do we need a new email address?

No. Keep support@yourcompany.com exactly as it is. Relay Desk connects to it; it doesn't replace it.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are up and running in under fifteen minutes: forward your inbox, invite your team, and you're already looking at a queue.

What will it cost?

We haven't locked in pricing yet. Teams who join during early access keep a preferential rate for as long as they stay with us.

Is this only for support inboxes?

Not at all. It works for any small team sharing an inbox: support, sales, ops, or that one "hello@" address everyone dreads opening.

What happens when we outgrow "small"?

We're building Relay Desk to grow with your team. It's designed first for teams of two to twenty; if you're bigger than that, we'd still like to talk.

Is our data safe?

Security and privacy are core to how we're building this from day one. Full details on our data handling will be published before general launch.

When can we start?

We're bringing on early access teams a few at a time, so we can build this closely with each one. Request access below and we'll follow up personally.
